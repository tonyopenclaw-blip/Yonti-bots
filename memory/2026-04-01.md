### Trading Strategies (4-Scenario Engine)
1. **DEEP BUY**: YES < $0.15 → buy YES (penny odds, high reward)
2. **DRIFT BUY**: YES $0.35-$0.45 → mean reversion bounce
3. **DRIFT SHORT**: YES $0.55-$0.65 → sell overpriced contracts
4. **SNIPE SHORT**: YES > $0.75 → removed (mathematically proven losing)

### Strategy Details
- DEEP strategies: ride to contract expiry, NO stop loss
- DRIFT strategies: TP +25%, SL -15%
- Time minimums: 5-8 minutes
- Entry logic: cointegration filter (BTC+ETH both above $0.55 = bullish)
- Kelly Criterion for position sizing
